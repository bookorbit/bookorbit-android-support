BookOrbit Android

Support

How can we help?

Get help

Ask a question, report a reproducible problem, or suggest a feature by opening an issue.

Include the app's build number (shown under More > About), your phone and its Android version, your BookOrbit server version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Do not post passwords, access tokens, or private server addresses.

Connection problems

Confirm that your BookOrbit server address opens in the phone's browser, and that the server is reachable from outside its local network when you are away from home. For a server on your home network, allow BookOrbit to find devices on local networks when Android asks. If someone else operates the server, contact that administrator about account access, certificates, or availability.

Premium purchases

Open BookOrbit Premium in the app and select Restore purchases. Google Play must use the Google Account that made the purchase.

Manage a yearly subscription in Google Play subscriptions. Google Play handles billing, cancellations, and refunds.

Crash reports

The app sends crash reports, with private details removed, so problems can be fixed. Turn them off at any time in the app's Settings.

Privacy and security

Read the Privacy Policy. Report a security concern privately through GitHub Security Advisories, not in a public issue.
